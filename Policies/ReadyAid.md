# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 26, 2026**

## Introduction

ReadyAid ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("App") handles your information.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device
Our App stores the following data exclusively on your device:
- Training progress (completed levels, scores, stars)
- Achievements and certificates earned
- App usage preferences

This data is stored using Apple's standard local storage mechanisms (UserDefaults) and is not transmitted to any external server.

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Personal identification information** (name, email, phone number, address)
- **Location data**
- **Contacts or address book**
- **Photos or media files**
- **Health or medical information**
- **Device identifiers** (beyond what is necessary for basic app functionality)

## How We Use Your Information

All data is processed locally on your device to:
- Track your first aid training progress
- Display achievements and certificates
- Persist app preferences and settings

We do not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties.

## Third-Party Services

Our App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, data security is managed by your device's built-in security features (e.g., device passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, iOS sandboxing). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date to benefit from the latest security updates.

## Your Rights

You have full control over your data:
- All training data is stored locally and can be cleared by deleting the App
- You can reset your progress at any time within the App settings

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App and updating the "Last updated" date.
