SpatialSage ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information.

### 1. Information We Collect

SpatialSage accesses the following device sensors and data solely for in-app training functionality:

- **Location Services**: Used for spatial awareness training. Location data is processed in real-time on your device and is never stored, transmitted, or shared.
- **Magnetometer (Compass)**: Used for direction-finding training exercises.
- **Motion Sensors**: Used for orientation and movement-based training.

### 2. Data Storage

- **All training data (scores, progress, streaks) is stored locally on your device only.**
- We do NOT collect, upload, or store any personal data on external servers.
- We do NOT use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.
- We do NOT require account registration.

### 3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose any of your data to third parties. Since all data remains on your device, there is no data for us to share.

### 4. Children's Privacy

SpatialSage does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

### 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the updated version available within the app.
