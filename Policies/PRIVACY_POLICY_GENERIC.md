# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 4, 2026

**We** ("we," "our," or "us") respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile applications (each an "App").

## 1. Data Collection and Storage

**Our Apps are designed with privacy in mind.** We generally prioritize local storage and do not collect, store, or transmit your personal content (such as documents, photos, or recordings) to our servers unless explicitly stated for a specific cloud-based feature. For most of our Apps, all data you create is stored locally on your device or in your personal iCloud container.

## 2. Permissions and Usage

To provide its functionality, the App may request access to certain features on your device. Access is only requested when necessary for a specific feature. You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings.

*   **Photo Library / Camera:** If the App supports image features, we may request access to your photo library or camera to allow you to add photos to your content or save images.
*   **Microphone:** If the App offers audio recording or voice features, we may request access to the microphone. Audio data is processed locally on your device unless otherwise specified.
*   **Face ID / Touch ID:** If the App offers biometric security, we may use it to lock/unlock the App. We do not have access to your biometric data; the authentication process is handled entirely by the iOS system.
*   **Location:** If the App requires location data for a specific feature (e.g., tagging a location), we may request access. We do not track your location in the background for advertising purposes.
*   **Notifications:** We may request permission to send local notifications to remind you of tasks or events you have scheduled.

## 3. Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that track you across other apps and websites for marketing purposes.

If an App includes optional integration with third-party services (e.g., AI assistants, cloud storage), data will only be sent to those services if you actively enable the feature and authorize the connection.

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Since most data is stored locally on your device, its security largely depends on your device's security. We recommend using a device passcode or biometric authentication to protect your information.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
