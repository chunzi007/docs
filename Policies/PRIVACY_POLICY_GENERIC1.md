# Privacy Policy
**Effective Date:** May 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **SightTrainer** ("the App") handles information when you use it.

## 1. Data Collection
SightTrainer is designed to work **without an account** and **without collecting personal data**.
We do not operate servers that receive your gameplay data.

The App may store certain information **locally on your device** to provide core functionality, such as:
- Game progress and settings
- Daily challenge records
- Achievements and statistics

## 2. Permissions
SightTrainer may request the following permission only when you use the related feature:
- **Photo Library**: Used only when you create a custom level from your photos. Images are processed on your device.

You can manage permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

## 3. Third-Party Services
SightTrainer does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not use third-party analytics that track you across apps.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
SightTrainer is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will address the request.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be available at the same link.
