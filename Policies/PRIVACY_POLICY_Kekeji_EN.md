# Kekeji Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-03-30

Welcome to “Kekeji” (the “App”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, why we process it, what third parties may receive it, your choices and rights, and how to contact us.

This Policy applies to your use of the App on iOS devices.

## 1. Information We Process

### 1.1 Data you create in the App (primarily stored on your device)

The App’s core features (subscriptions/recurring bills management, billing calendar, budget, insights, import/export) are primarily processed and stored locally on your device. Data you create or import may include:

- Accounts (e.g., account name, currency)
- Categories (e.g., category name, icon, color)
- Records/entries (amount, time, account, category, note)
- Subscriptions/recurring bills (name, amount, recurrence, start date, enabled status)
- Budget settings

Such data is typically stored in the App sandbox on your device. We do not upload your ledger content to our servers unless you explicitly choose to export/import or use system backup features.

### 1.2 Device and network information (for ads, attribution, and anti-fraud)

To provide advertising, attribution, anti-fraud, and service stability, we or third-party SDKs integrated in the App (see Section 4) may process device/network-related information, such as:

- Device identifiers (e.g., IDFA when you grant permission; or other identifiers used/generated by third-party SDKs)
- Device information (model, OS version, language, time zone)
- Network information (IP address, carrier, network type)
- Ad event data (ad requests, impressions, clicks, video playback, failure reasons)

The exact scope depends on the third-party SDKs’ behavior and their privacy policies/data disclosures.

### 1.3 App runtime information (stability and performance)

To improve stability and user experience, we or third-party SDKs may process runtime information such as crash logs, performance data, load times, and error logs for troubleshooting and optimization.

### 1.4 Network requests and remote configuration

The App may request remote configuration related to advertising (e.g., whether ads are enabled, placement parameters). These requests typically do not include your ledger content.

## 2. Permissions and System Prompts

### 2.1 App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

When needed, the App may request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission for advertising attribution and to reduce irrelevant ads.

- You may allow or deny this permission.
- Denying permission does not affect the App’s core features.
- You can change this permission in iOS Settings at any time.

### 2.2 File access (backup import/export)

When you use backup export/import, the App uses the system file picker to read/write files you select. This happens only when you initiate the action, for backup and data migration purposes.

## 3. How We Use Information

We process information to:

- Provide and maintain core features (subscriptions/recurring bills management, billing calendar, budget, insights, import/export)
- Show ads, measure attribution, and optimize delivery (within your permission scope)
- Prevent abuse and improve security (anti-fraud)
- Improve stability and performance (diagnostics and troubleshooting)

## 4. Third-Party SDKs/Services

The App may integrate third-party SDKs/services for advertising, attribution, anti-fraud, and stability. The current version may include (but is not limited to):

- Ad mediation and advertising services: AnyThink SDK
- Ad network components: Pangle/CSJ (Bytedance-related components)
- Attribution-related components: Smartdigimkt SDK

Third-party SDKs may process device identifiers, network information, ad event data, and performance data according to their own policies. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies and disclosures.

## 5. Sharing, Transfers, and Public Disclosure

### 5.1 Sharing

We may share necessary information:

- With third-party SDK/service providers for ads, attribution, anti-fraud, and stability (limited to necessary device/network/ad event data)
- When required by law, regulation, or governmental/judicial requests

### 5.2 Transfers and public disclosure

We do not transfer or publicly disclose your personal information to third parties unless you explicitly consent or as otherwise required by law.

## 6. Storage and Security

- Ledger data is typically stored locally in the App sandbox on your device
- We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to reduce risks of unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction
- You should protect your device and Apple ID (e.g., passcode/Face ID) to prevent unauthorized access

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

- Manage ATT permission in iOS Settings
- Manage backups via export/import
- Delete locally stored App data by uninstalling the App (backup files you saved outside the App sandbox must be deleted by you)

## 8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 14. If you are a minor, please use the App with the consent and guidance of a guardian.
