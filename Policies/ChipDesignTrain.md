# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: May 27, 2026*

Thank you for using **ChipClass**. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

> **Key Point:** ChipClass does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All app data stays entirely on your device.

---

## 1. Information We Do *Not* Collect

We do **not** collect any of the following:

- **Personal identifiers** — name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
- **Location data** — precise or approximate GPS location.
- **Device identifiers** — IDFA, IDFV, IP address, or any unique device ID.
- **Usage analytics** — no analytics, tracking, or behavioral profiling.
- **Photos, contacts, or calendar data.**
- **Payment or financial information.**

## 2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

ChipClass stores your **learning progress** exclusively on your device using Apple's built-in storage (UserDefaults). This data includes:

- Which courses and challenges you have completed (anonymized course IDs).
- Total stars earned, daily streak count, and study time.
- Your quiz answer history (correct/incorrect per question ID).

**This data:**

- Never leaves your device.
- Is not linked to your identity in any way.
- Can be completely erased at any time using the **"Reset Progress"** button in the Profile tab.
- Is used solely to personalize your learning experience (unlock new stages, track achievements).

## 3. No Internet Transmission

ChipClass is a **fully offline application**. The app does not make any network requests, does not connect to any servers, and does not transmit any data over the internet. All content (lessons, quizzes, code examples) is built into the app and runs entirely on your device.

## 4. No Third-Party Services

ChipClass does **not** integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or social media platforms. The app is built exclusively with Apple's native frameworks (SwiftUI, Foundation, Combine).

## 5. No Advertising

ChipClass displays **no advertisements**. We do not use ad networks, and we do not personalize or serve any ads.

## 6. Children's Privacy

ChipClass does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal information at all, parental consent is not required for use of this app.

## 7. Your Data Rights

Under data protection laws such as GDPR (EU) and CCPA (California), you have rights regarding your personal data. Since ChipClass does not collect any personal data:

- There is **no personal data** to access, correct, delete, or export.
- Your learning progress is stored **only on your device** and can be deleted by using the in-app Reset button or by uninstalling the app.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
