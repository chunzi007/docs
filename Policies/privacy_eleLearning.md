# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

EleLearning ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

**TL;DR**: We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. All your training progress stays on your device.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Do NOT Provide

EleLearning does **not** require you to create an account, provide an email address, or submit any personal information to use the app.

### 2.2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored **exclusively on your device** using Apple's AppStorage and is never transmitted to any server:

- Training statistics (levels cleared, streak count, last cleared date)
- Quiz statistics (total questions answered, correct count, quiz streak)
- App settings (e.g., haptic feedback preference)

This data is used solely to display your progress and personalize your experience within the app.

### 2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
- We do **not** track your location.
- We do **not** use analytics, crash reporting, or any third-party SDKs that collect data.
- We do **not** display advertisements.
- We do **not** have any server backend that receives data from the app.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no data to use beyond what is necessary to provide the app's core functionality — logic gate training and quiz practice. All processing happens entirely on your device.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do **not** share, sell, rent, or trade any information with third parties. There is no data to share because no data is collected.

## 5. Data Security

All data is stored locally on your device and is protected by the security mechanisms provided by iOS. We do not transmit any data over the internet. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date to ensure you have the latest security protections.

## 6. Children's Privacy

EleLearning is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Since no personal data is collected, the app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
