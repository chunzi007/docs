# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 4, 2026

**We** ("we," "our," or "us") respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile applications (each an "App").

## 1. Data Collection and Storage

**Our Apps are local-first applications.** We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal content (such as diary entries, documents, scans, photos, or audio recordings) to our servers. All data you create is stored locally on your device. We do not request or use your location.

## 2. Permissions and Usage

To provide its functionality, the App may request access to certain features on your device. You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings.

*   **Photo Library / Camera:** We request access to your photo library or camera to allow you to add photos/scans to your content and to save images from the App.
*   **Face ID / Touch ID:** We use biometric authentication to lock and secure your content. We do not have access to your biometric data; the authentication process is handled entirely by the iOS system.
*   **Microphone / Speech Recognition:** Not requested. Voice input features are removed.
*   **Location:** Not requested. Users can manually input location information if desired.

## 3. Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs that track you across other apps and websites.
If you enable optional AI assist by configuring your own service endpoint in Settings, text you choose to send will be transmitted to that service. This feature is disabled by default.

## 4. Data Security

Since your data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's security. We recommend using a passcode or Face ID/Touch ID to protect your device and enabling the App's built-in lock feature.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
