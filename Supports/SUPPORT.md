## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Where is my data stored?
Our apps are **local-first**. All your documents, scans, and entries are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your data to any servers.

### 2. How do I backup my data?
Since data is stored locally, we recommend regularly backing up your device using iCloud Backup or iTunes/Finder. Some of our apps also offer specific export or backup features in the Settings menu.

### 3. Can I recover deleted items?
If you delete an item within the app, it may be permanently removed depending on the specific app's implementation. Please check the "Recently Deleted" folder if available, or restore from a device backup.

### 4. How do I request a refund?
All purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. To request a refund, please visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and sign in with your Apple ID.
