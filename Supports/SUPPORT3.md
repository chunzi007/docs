## FAQ
### 1. Do I need an account?
No. SightTrainer does not require login.

### 2. Where is my data stored?
Your progress, stats, and settings are stored locally on your device.

### 3. Why does the App request Photo Library permission?
Only the **Custom Levels** feature needs Photo Library access to let you select photos. Photos are processed on your device.

### 4. How do I report a bug?
Email us with:
- Your device model and iOS version
- What you expected vs what happened
- Screenshots or a screen recording (if possible)
